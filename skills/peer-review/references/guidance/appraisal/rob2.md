# RoB 2: trial-result appraisal guide

Selected guide to the five domains, not a replacement for the official signalling questions and algorithms. Sterne JAC et al., BMJ 2019;366:l4898 ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l4898)). Requirements paraphrased; formal assessment requires the linked tool.

Source: [Official source](https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/rob-2-0-tool).

Assess a specific outcome, time point and effect estimate. Specify whether the target is assignment to intervention or adherence; domain 2 differs. The core version covers individually randomised parallel trials; use the official cluster/crossover variant for those designs.

| Domain | What to assess |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Randomisation | Check sequence randomness, concealment until enrolment/assignment, and whether baseline imbalance suggests allocation problems. |
| 2 Intervention deviations | Follow the selected effect-of-interest pathway: awareness, trial-context deviations, balance between groups, adherence/co-interventions and appropriate analysis. |
| 3 Missing outcomes | Assess availability, evidence against consequential missingness bias, and dependence of missingness on true outcomes. |
| 4 Outcome measurement | Assess measurement suitability, comparable ascertainment, assessor awareness and whether knowledge could influence assessment. |
| 5 Reported-result selection | Compare with the prespecified analysis plan; assess selection among eligible outcome measurements and analyses. |

Use the official tool to answer conditional signalling questions and derive domain judgments. Preserve evidence and reasons for overrides. Overall: low risk requires all domains low; some concerns applies without high-risk domains; high risk follows any high domain or multiple concerns that substantially reduce confidence. No summed score. Lack of blinding alone does not establish high risk.
