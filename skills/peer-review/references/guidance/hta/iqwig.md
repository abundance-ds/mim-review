# IQWiG General Methods 8.0: review guide

Selected guide to benefit assessment and health economics in German HTA; not a complete IQWiG checklist. Institute for Quality and Efficiency in Health Care. [DOI:10.60584/General-Methods_V8.0](https://doi.org/10.60584/General-Methods_V8.0). Factual section guide; source rights retained.

Source: [Official source](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf).

Select the commission and legal context first. Benefit assessment under §35a and economic assessment under §35b have different purposes; do not assume NICE rules apply.

| Manual section | Review focus |
| --- | --- |
| [§§3.1–3.2](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=65) | Check patient-relevant benefits/harms, surrogate validity, outcome-specific certainty, missing information and adequate follow-up. |
| [§3.3.3](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=84) | Match approved indication, population and the specified appropriate comparator for drug benefit assessment. |
| [§§3.5–3.6](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=94) | For diagnostics/screening, distinguish accuracy from demonstrated patient benefit along the care pathway. |
| [§§4.3–4.6](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=111) | Justify population, comparators, perspective, horizon, evaluation type, clinical inputs and utilities where used. |
| [§§4.7–4.8](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=117) | Check relevant costs, quantities, prices, inflation adjustment and discounting. |
| [§§4.9–4.12](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=122) | Assess model structure/validation, disaggregated and incremental results, uncertainty and interpretation of ICERs. |
| [§§4.13–4.15](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=130) | Apply budget-impact requirements and distinguish economic evaluations outside §35b. |
| [§9](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=193) | Assess reproducible retrieval, selection, completeness and trustworthiness of evidence. |
| [§10](https://www.iqwig.de/methoden/general-methods_version-8-0.pdf#page=206) | Apply relevant qualitative/quantitative synthesis and bias methods; inspect the applicable subsection. |

Open the cited section for exact rules and exceptions before criticising a departure. This selected guide supports peer review, not a claim of regulatory compliance.

When this guide informs a review, include a visible summary/limitations note: “This review used selected IQWiG (Germany) methods; it was not a full HTA assessment.” Give the edition and link to the manual sections actually consulted. Combine with the other jurisdiction’s note if both were used.
