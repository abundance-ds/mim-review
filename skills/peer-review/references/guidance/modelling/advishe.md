# AdViSHE model validation assessment

All 13 assessment items, restated with original IDs. Vemer P et al., PharmacoEconomics 34:349–361, Figure 4. Source retains its rights; factual requirements are paraphrased, with optional illustrative examples omitted.

Source: [Official source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40273-015-0327-2).

| Item | Assessment question |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | Was conceptual-model suitability reviewed by experts? Identify them, justify expertise, report agreement; if not, explain why. |
| A2 | Was the conceptual model compared with published models or clinical descriptions? Locate the comparison, or explain its absence. |
| B1 | Were input data reviewed by experts? Identify expertise and agreement on appropriateness, or explain absence of review. |
| B2 | Where regression-derived inputs are used, were model-fit tests conducted? Report methods, rationale and results, or explain why not. |
| C1 | Did experts review the implemented model? Identify expertise and independence; locate results and unresolved issues, or explain absence. |
| C2 | Were extreme parameter combinations tested? Locate settings and outcomes, or explain why not. |
| C3 | Were individual paths or cohort traces checked for logical correctness? Locate tests/results, or explain absence. |
| C4 | Were modules unit-tested against a predefined protocol, criteria and acceptance limits? Locate tests/results, or explain absence. |
| D1 | Were model outcomes reviewed by experts? Identify expertise and agreement on plausibility, or explain absence. |
| D2 | Were outputs compared with other models? State whether full models or only published results were available; explain differences and comparability; locate results, or explain absence. |
| D3 | Were results checked using alternative input datasets? Locate tests/results, or explain absence. |
| D4 | Were outputs compared with empirical data? Distinguish summary versus patient data; explain discrepancies and locate results. D4.A: data used in model development; D4.B: independent data. Explain absent validation. |
| E1 | Were other validation techniques applied? Describe them and locate their results. |

Before assessment, locate the conceptual assumptions; input searches, sources and selection rationale; uncertainty distributions, missing-data handling, transformations and calibration; and differences between conceptual and implemented models. Do not calculate a total validation score. A justified non-applicable answer is allowed; reported checks are not checks performed by the reviewing agent.
