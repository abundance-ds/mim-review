# SRQR 2014 checklist

Qualitative research across approaches. Requirements restated concisely; original item numbers retained where the source numbers them. Match applicable items to manuscript evidence; reporting omissions do not establish poor study conduct.

Source: [SRQR 2014 checklist](https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000388) · [Checklist source](https://links.lww.com/ACADMED/A218).

| Item / topic | Reporting requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify topic and qualitative approach in title. |
| 2 | Summarise purpose, methods, findings, implications. |
| 3 | Explain problem, significance, existing theory/evidence. |
| 4 | Specify purpose and questions. |
| 5 | Describe and justify the qualitative approach; include guiding theory where appropriate. Identifying the research paradigm is recommended. |
| 6 | Discuss researcher attributes, relationships, assumptions, influence. |
| 7 | Describe and justify setting/context. |
| 8 | Justify sampling and stopping criteria. |
| 9 | Report ethics/consent or exceptions, confidentiality, security. |
| 10 | Justify collection procedures, timing, iteration, triangulation, modifications. |
| 11 | Describe collection instruments/devices and changes. |
| 12 | Report units, characteristics, participation. |
| 13 | Explain transcription, management, verification, coding, de-identification. |
| 14 | Explain how findings were developed, who analysed the data, the analytic approach and its rationale. |
| 15 | Justify credibility/trustworthiness procedures. |
| 16 | Present themes, inferences, interpretations, models. |
| 17 | Support findings with empirical examples. |
| 18 | Relate findings to scholarship, contributions, implications, transferability. |
| 19 | Discuss limitations and trustworthiness. |
| 20 | Disclose conflicts and management. |
| 21 | Report funding/support and funder involvement. |

Original items 1–21; sometimes cited as S1–S21. Select techniques appropriate to the qualitative paradigm; do not treat member checking or saturation as universal requirements.
