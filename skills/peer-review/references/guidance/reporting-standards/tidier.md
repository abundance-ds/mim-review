# TIDieR 2014 checklist

Intervention descriptions, including comparators. Requirements restated concisely; original item numbers retained where the source numbers them. Match applicable items to manuscript evidence; reporting omissions do not establish poor study conduct.

Source: [TIDieR 2014 checklist](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.g1687) · [Checklist source](https://www.equator-network.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/TIDieR-Checklist-Word.docx).

| Item / topic | Reporting requirement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Name the intervention. |
| 2 | Explain its rationale, theory, and essential goals. |
| 3 | Identify materials for participants, providers, and training; give access locations. |
| 4 | Specify procedures, activities, processes, and supporting activities. |
| 5 | For each provider category, report expertise, background, and intervention-specific training. |
| 6 | Specify delivery channels and individual/group delivery. |
| 7 | Describe delivery settings and necessary infrastructure. |
| 8 | Give sessions, schedule, duration, intensity/dose, and overall delivery period. |
| 9 | If tailoring was planned, specify what, why, when, and how. |
| 10 | If modified during the study, specify what changed, why, when, and how. |
| 11 | If fidelity/adherence was assessed, describe methods, assessors, and strategies to maintain or improve delivery. |
| 12 | If assessed, report actual fidelity/adherence. |

Items 10 and 12 concern completed delivery and do not apply to protocols. Describe each intervention and comparator separately. Materials may be supplied through a stable supplement or link; do not demand duplication in the main text.
