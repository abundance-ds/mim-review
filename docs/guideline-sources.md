Original wording is retained for the openly reusable checklists; other entries restate the factual requirements. Checklist entries preserve original item IDs and conditional requirements; selected guides explicitly identify their limits. SAMPL, JARS-Quant and budget-impact guidance use source headings instead of invented official numbers. JARS-Quant restates the requirements of general Table 1 (2020 revision), with links to specialised modules; ISPOR–SMDM includes all 91 numbered recommendations from the series overview. PRISMA and TRIPOD+AI include their abstract checklists.

Choose one primary reporting standard plus relevant extensions/components. Report evidence and omissions by item, not a numerical study-quality score. The ISPOR references concern methods and good practice; the modelling entry links to the overview of a seven-paper series. CHEERS does not cover budget impact analysis.

Useful checklist downloads: [STROBE](https://www.strobe-statement.org/checklists/), [SAMPL](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/sampl/), [PRISMA](https://www.prisma-statement.org/), [CONSORT/SPIRIT](https://www.consort-spirit.org/), [APA JARS](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/resources/apa-style-jars).
